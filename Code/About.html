About MotoGO

A simple bike-rental website made as a college project.

Who We Are

At MotoGO, we believe that every journey should be simple, affordable, and exciting. We are a bike rental platform designed to make it easy for people to find and rent the right bike for their travel needs.

Our Aim

The aim of this MotoGO is to make bike renting convenient, affordable, and accessible by allowing users to browse, compare, and book bikes online with ease.
